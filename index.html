FAQ

Why is ATS compliance important?

Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human sees them. Our resumes are designed with correct formatting and keywords to bypass these bots and get you shortlisted.

How does the Portfolio Website work?

With the Ultra Plan, we build a personal website for you showcasing your bio, skills, and resume. You get free hosting for 1 year. You can share this link on LinkedIn or with recruiters to stand out.

What is the turnaround time?

For the Pro Plan, we deliver the first draft within 24 hours. For the Ultra Plan, we prioritize your order and deliver within 12 hours. Revisions are usually done within a few hours.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, we offer 1 free revision. You can request changes to the layout or content after receiving the first draft.
